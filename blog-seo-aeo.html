Where SEO and AEO meet

Visibility in 2026 = rankings + citations.
SEO
Search Engine Optimisation
What it optimises for:
Rankings on search results pages
Backlinks and domain authority
Keywords and meta tags
Page speed and technical performance
Click-through rate and traffic
AEO
Answer Engine Optimisation
What it optimises for:
Citations in AI-generated answers
Featured snippets and answer boxes
FAQ schema and structured data
Clear, direct answers to questions
Entity recognition
Where they
overlap

Search is no longer only a list of blue links. In 2026, visibility is shaped by both traditional rankings and AI-generated answers that pull from trusted sources.

SEO still matters, but it is no longer the whole story

SEO helps your content appear in search results and earn clicks. AEO helps your expertise appear in answer engines, AI summaries and citation-heavy experiences.

The strongest brands are not choosing one approach over the other. They are building content that is visible in search and useful enough to be cited as the answer.

What both strategies need

  • High-quality, authoritative content built around real user intent.
  • Clear topical expertise with well-structured explanations.
  • Regular updates that reflect current information and customer questions.
  • Good technical foundations, including mobile performance and fast loading times.
  • Content designed to answer direct questions in plain, confident language.

The real goal

SEO gets you discovered. AEO helps you become the answer. Together, they create a stronger digital presence: one that is both searchable and quotable.

Visibility in 2026 is not just about being found. It is about being trusted enough to be cited.
